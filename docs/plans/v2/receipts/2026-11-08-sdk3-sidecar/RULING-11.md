## Verdict: **B — ONE shared validator pair in `loremaster/store/_txn` that checks and never converts. The builder's reading is right and S2 is honoured, not bent. Four riders, one of them a found defect.**

- **Why B and not A, by the two laws that bind here.** S2's words were about COERCION: six hand-rolled conversions duplicating what the SDK decodes natively (PreciseDatetime, tz-aware UTC, nanoseconds kept — measured), "not merged into a seventh shared copy" meaning *no seventh converter*. A validator that converts nothing is not a coercer; it is the guard the SDK does not supply (the decoder cannot know a column is REQUIRED). What is left after S2 is a POLICY — "a required stamp column holds a tz-aware datetime, else refuse by name" — and global law is unambiguous about policies shared by call sites: a function they call, never a pattern they clone. Reading A would mint six (in truth EIGHT — `keeps` and `principal_keys` read stamps too) copies of one validation rule, which is the #102/#120 shape whose receipts fill this repo's CLAUDE.md. `_txn` is the right home (the store seam; `record_key` is the precedent beside it); `lorerunes` is for cross-MEMBER policy and only `loremaster` reads store rows.
- **Why "never converts" is load-bearing, not pedantry (and why the validator must run BEFORE the pydantic model).** The builder derived from the DDL that every column these read is `datetime` / `option<datetime>`, so a non-datetime value can only mean schema drift or a corrupt row — exactly what must be LOUD. But pydantic's default `datetime` field COERCES an ISO string silently; a mapper that skipped the helper and let the model do the typing would re-introduce S2's forbidden conversion through the back door, invisibly. So the helper is the chokepoint. Alternative chokepoint the operator may prefer instead of (not beside) the helper's type check: `strict=True` on the row models' datetime fields — then the helper's job shrinks to naming row/column in the refusal. Either is one implementation; both at once is two enforcements of one rule and needs the "one place enforces" question answered first. Recommend: the helper, because it is already wired to 24 sites and carries the message.
